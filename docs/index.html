About

I'm Hubert Thieriot, a data scientist who works with the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air (CREA), Ambitious Impact and Founders Pledge.

I started the Effective Advocacy Project to help bring more evidence and insight to the advocacy I care about — using data, machine learning and automation to surface the signals that sharpen communications and public affairs. If that sounds useful for your work, get in touch.
