<p>I design and prototype data products that help advocacy teams understand how issues arise, who carries narratives forward, and where strategic opportunities appear. The work here explores workflows for narrative framing analysis, actor mapping, and dissemination tracking.
If you would like to collaborate, riff on these ideas, or stress-test an approach on your own corpus, feel free to <a href="mailto:hubert.thieriot@gmail.com">get in touch</a>.</p>

<p>All tooling is part of the <strong>EFI corpus</strong> project: a collection of pipelines, annotation workflows, and visual reports that leverage large language models alongside classical NLP to deliver reproducible analysis.</p>
