Transportation and Traffic Emissions

Air pollution caused by emissions from vehicles such as cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses, and related road traffic, including road dust resuspension and tire wear.

Industrial and Power Plant Emissions

Air pollution caused by emissions from factories, manufacturing, industrial fuel combustion, coal-fired power plants, and mining operations.

Biomass Burning and Agricultural Emissions

Air pollution caused by burning of biomass for land clearing, agricultural practices, slash-and-burn, deforestation, and wildfires.

Household Fuel and Cooking Emissions

Air pollution caused by household use of fossil fuels or biomass for cooking and heating.

Waste Burning and Disposal Emissions

Air pollution caused by the open burning of waste, including municipal solid waste, landfill fires, and neighborhood trash burning.

Construction and Urban Dust Emissions

Air pollution caused by construction activities, roadworks, and resuspended soil or dust, especially in urban settings.

Microplastics and Synthetic Particle Emissions

Air pollution caused by microplastics and synthetic particles released from tire wear, plastic degradation, and related sources.
